📖 How to Play

🎯 Goal

Win your College season, earn a National Championship, get drafted, then win the Pro Super Bowl. Or just play one mode — your call. The Mashup Career links both: your college stars carry into the pros.

🏈 On Offense (you are the QB)

← ↑ ↓ → or WASD — move / scramble  •  Space — throw to the most open receiver  •  Enter — snap the ball  •  1/2/3 — pick a play before the snap  •  After a catch you control the receiver — run for the end zone! Don't cross the line of scrimmage before throwing.

🛡 On Defense

Move your linebacker with ←↑↓→. Space = dive tackle (burst of speed). Chase the ball carrier and make the tackle before the end zone. Pick 1/2/3 for Man / Zone / Blitz before the snap.

📋 Rules (simplified Retro Bowl)

4 downs to gain 10 yards. Score TDs (6 + auto XP), field goals (3), safeties (2). 4th down: punt or try a field goal if close (timing mini-game). Clock runs during plays. Quarters are short — every play matters!

🎓 College extras

Recruit new stars each week, chase the Heisman with your QB, survive the conference race + playoff, then enter the Draft. Your draftees boost your Pro team in Mashup mode.

📱 Mobile

On-screen D-pad + THROW/SNAP buttons appear on touch devices.
